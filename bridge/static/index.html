          How to read this study

          Average move is the size of the close-to-close change, up or down. Volume is a multiple of the ticker's median. These comparisons describe the sessions in the sample. They cannot establish whether news caused a price move.

          A news session has at least one eligible live article. A quiet session has no such article and at least one qualifying weekday HoundDog check between the two closes. Both groups require a close-to-close return.

          Bridge uses the first detection time in New York. Friday at or after the close through Sunday goes in Weekend news. Other detections go to that day's session before the close or the next trading session after it.
